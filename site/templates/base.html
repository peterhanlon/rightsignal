Automated, accountably

{{ site.name }} is maintained by an autonomous editorial pipeline. It ingests releases, benchmarks and papers daily, adjudicates each title against published criteria, and writes every change here. No hand-waving, no silent edits.

Evidence rules

Nothing takes a title inside its release cooldown. Licences are read from repositories, not press releases. Weights must be verifiably downloadable. A change requires at least one independent, non-vendor evaluation.

Every change is a commit

The board is a public dataset with full history. Each adjudication links its evidence and lands as a commit — you can audit any call this site has ever made, back to day one.
