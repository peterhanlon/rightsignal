Every title's lineage now runs back to 2023 — who held it, from when, and why
it changed hands, from GPT-4's long unbroken reign to Claude 3 Opus ending it
in a weekend, from Bark's expressive-but-unsteerable era to Kokoro running on
a CPU. Each transition is a dated entry below, marked retrospective, with the
official release linked — open weights always to the original repository.
Where a lineage opens on 1 August 2023, that's where our records begin, not
when the first holder took the crown.
