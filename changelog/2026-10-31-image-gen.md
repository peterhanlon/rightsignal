GPT Image 2 has lost this title to its own stablemate. On Artificial Analysis's text-to-image arena, today's standings put GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst (max) first on 1,222, with GPT Image 2.5 Flare (max) second on 1,207 and GPT Image 2 (high) third on 1,186 — Sunburst has climbed from second place in the previous reading, so this is a sustained move rather than a one-off blip.

The Arena boards say the same thing. Sunburst tops text-to-image on 1421 against GPT Image 2's 1381, and single-image edit on 1520 against 1461, where the former pick is now third behind Flare. On Artificial Analysis's editing arena Sunburst leads on 1,167, with Flare on 1,145, MAI-Image-2.6 on 1,124 and GPT Image 2 (high) down in fifth on 1,114. That is both principal criteria — generation quality and editability — going the same way on two independent boards.

The Arena scores are still flagged preliminary on a few thousand votes, which is why we waited; Artificial Analysis's larger samples now corroborate, and the ordering has held across readings.

Nothing in the criteria blocks the change: Sunburst is generally available on the same OpenAI API, and at $210.70 per 1,000 images it is fractionally cheaper than GPT Image 2's $211.00. If you are editing at volume, price is still the argument against both: MAI-Image-2.6 is listed at $38.90 per 1,000 images and sits third on the editing arena, and Nano Banana 2 at $67. For outright quality, Sunburst is now the one to beat.
