Fish Audio Drama 3 would take this title on today's evidence, but the case has not yet cleared our bar for a change, so it is recorded as the challenger for now. The adjudicator's reasoning:

The title moves to Fish Audio Drama 3. The claim that put Breeze TTS 2 here — top open-weight model on the Artificial Analysis provider-voice arena at 1202, 82 points clear — no longer stands. On the live board Breeze sits at 1229 and is third among open weights, behind Drama 3 (1275 on 15 November, 1277 on 22 November) and Drama 3 Preview (1239). Drama 3 also appears in the board's overall top ten at #8 in the 22 November snapshot, so this is a sustained reading rather than a one-off.

On licence, Drama 3 is the less restrictive of the two: Breeze's weights are research and non-commercial only, while Fish Audio's Community Licence v1.0 is free commercially below US$500k annual revenue. It is not a clean licence — it grants no right to offer the model to others as a TTS, voice-cloning or inference service at any revenue — so get a written agreement from Fish Audio before building an API product on it.

The real cost is hardware. Drama 3 is a 35B-parameter MoE with roughly 3B active per token, about 40 GB of weights on disk and self-hosting guidance of around 48 GB of VRAM, against Breeze's practical 12 GB floor. If you cannot find those cards, this is not an upgrade.

The picture is board-specific. On the controlled-voice arena, Artificial Analysis named Ant's Ming-3.0-tts the open-weight leader on 22 October and we could not confirm Drama 3 has overtaken it. MIT-licensed Ming-3.0-tts (1182 on provider-voice) remains the cleanest route for unrestricted commercial self-hosting; Step Audio EditX (1100) is the lighter fallback.
