Artificial Analysis's model table now shows Kimi K3.1 Pro at #6 with an Intelligence Index of 46, thirteen points ahead of DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 (Max) at 33 and GLM-5.3 (Max) at 32 ([aa-models](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models)). On raw benchmark quality that is a clear gap, and it is the first time anything plausibly open-weight has pulled meaningfully away from the current holder since the v5.0 re-basing.

We are not moving the title on it yet. This slot requires weights actually released, a workable licence and a defensible serving cost, and we have evidence for none of those three for K3.1 Pro — the AA table mixes closed and open models, so a listing there says nothing about whether the checkpoint is downloadable or on what terms. Nor do we have a cost-per-Index-task figure to set against DeepSeek's roughly $0.60, which is the main reason the title sits where it does.

Everything else ingested this week is irrelevant to the slot. The new #1, Claude Fable 5.2 (Max) at 65, plus the other Fable variants and Gemini 4 Carbon (High) at 60, are closed-weight. The Hugging Face trending entries are a quantised 4-bit Qwen derivative, a text-to-image model, a speculative-decoding draft model and a weight-edited 27B fine-tune — none are candidate base models here.

DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 keeps the title on the combination of index score, MIT licensing and cost. If a released-weights confirmation and a cost figure land for Kimi K3.1 Pro, this changes quickly.
