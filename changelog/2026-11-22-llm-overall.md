Gemini 4 Carbon (High) held this slot on the strength of leading the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. It no longer does. On the live v5.0.1 board read on 22 November, Claude Fable 5.2 (Max) is first on 65, with Fable 5.2 at xhigh and high on 62 alongside the new GPT-6.2 Astra configurations; Carbon (High) sits on 60 and is outside the top five ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models)). That ordering has held on the same board since Fable 5.2's 9 November release and through the 15 and 22 November fetches, so it is not a first-fetch artefact.

The lead is also no longer confined to one board. Vals AI's neutral mini-SWE-agent run of Terminal-Bench 4.0 now has Fable 5.2 tied with Claude Opus 5.5 at the top on 65.15%, and independent HLE trackers put Fable 5.2 first at roughly 73%. The official Terminal-Bench board remains unusable for cross-vendor ranking because each vendor runs in its own agent.

Two honest caveats. Carbon still leads Arena text, read first at about 1526 on 6 November and again on 13 November — but that entry is flagged preliminary on only a few thousand votes, and it is a single secondary measure against losses on reasoning, agentic and independent-benchmark criteria. And Fable 5.2 is expensive: $26.54 per index task, comfortably the dearest entry on the Artificial Analysis board, against $7.76 for Carbon. If cost per task matters more to you than the top of the reasoning boards, Carbon remains the better buy.

GPT-6.2 Astra (Max) entered Artificial Analysis at 62 on 22 November and is the next thing to watch.
