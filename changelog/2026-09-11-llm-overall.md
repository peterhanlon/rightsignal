Fable 5 keeps this title only on the Arena text board, and barely: on the 11 September fetch it sits at 1507 against Claude Opus 4.6 (high) on 1505 and Claude Fable 5.1 (max) on 1504, with a rank spread of 1-6. That is a tie band, not a lead.

Everywhere else its own successor is clear of it. On Artificial Analysis's re-based Intelligence Index (v4.3) Fable 5.1 shares first place on 53 while Fable 5 scores 50. On Vellum's composite (6 September) Fable 5.1 leads on 65% with Fable 5 outside the top ten. On the official Terminal-Bench v4.0 board Fable 5.1 scores 57.9%, Opus 5 51.8% and Fable 5 44.5% — the old 83.8% figure came from the retired 2.1 board and no longer describes current terminal work. Two independent boards plus the official agentic board agree, and the AA reading has held since the 7 September re-base, so this is corroboration rather than a single preliminary snapshot.

The boards do split at the top. GPT-6 Astra ties Fable 5.1 on the AA Index at 53, is much cheaper per Index task ($3.26 against $7.63), and leads Artificial Analysis's own Terminal-Bench v4.0 runs at 59.1% to 52.0%. But it has no Arena text placement and sits seventh on Vellum at 57.2%, so Fable 5.1 is ahead on more of this slot's criteria and takes the title. Astra becomes the live challenger; if it picks up a strong Arena placement it will have a case.
