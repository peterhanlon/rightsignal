The pick has run out of boards. On Artificial Analysis's text-to-video leaderboard as fetched today, Wan 3.0 is #1 on 1175 and Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 is #10 on 1128, behind Utopai X, MiniMax H3 (768p), Dreamina Seedance 2.5, FLUX 3 Max, HeyGen Video 1, MiniMax H3 Max, FLUX 3 and Dreamina Seedance 2.0 ([AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/video/leaderboard/text-to-video)).

That is not a one-board wobble. Wan 3.0 also leads text-to-video with audio (1242 to the pick's 1238) and without (1334 to 1325), and it tops the video editing board on 1197 with the pick third on 1121 — awkward, because step-by-step conversational editing was the pick's stated differentiator. The pick is fifth on image-to-video with audio (1179). Its one surviving lead, image-to-video without audio at 1365 to Wan 3.0's 1362, sits inside the margin.

On the slot's own criteria the hardware story matches. Independent same-seed testing puts Wan 3.0 at up to 30 seconds in a single job at 480p, 720p or 1080p with audio generated in the same pass, against Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash's 720p default (1080p and 4K served as upscales) extended in 10-second steps to a 40-second total ([Google](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-omni/)).

Two caveats carried forward. Wan 3.0 shows uncommanded camera cuts and identities bleeding when characters share the frame, so multi-character work still needs shot-by-shot checking. And the with-audio gap alone is four points on a ranked range of 1–2 — a tie; the case rests on the no-audio, editing and live text-to-video boards together.
