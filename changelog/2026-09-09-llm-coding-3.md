The agentic evidence has moved. Terminal-Bench 2.1 now has GPT-6 Astra first at 58.2%, with Fable 5.1 second at 57.9% and Claude Opus 5 third at 51.8% ([tbench.ai](https://www.tbench.ai/leaderboard/terminal-bench/2.1), 9 September). That is not a one-off: vals.ai's Terminal-Bench 2.1 table already had Astra top at 87.27% with Opus 5 fourth at 84.64%, so the same ordering holds across two boards and across the dossier's dates. On Arena WebDev, gpt-6-astra-max is first at 1796, 108 points clear of claude-opus-5-max at 1688 ([arena.ai](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/code/webdev), first seen 6 September, unchanged on 9 September).

Opus 5 still leads benchlm's independent SWE-bench Verified aggregate at 96%, ahead of Claude Mythos 5 (95.5%) and Fable 5 (95%). We are weighting that less than before: Astra is absent from the board rather than beaten on it, and vals has dropped SWE-bench Verified from its coding index as saturated — the top three sit within 1.5 points.

Practical notes. Astra is proprietary and priced at roughly $10/$50 per million tokens against Opus 5's $5/$25, so for high-volume repo-scale batch work where terminal-style autonomy matters less, Opus 5 remains the cheaper sensible choice. If a genuinely independent SWE-bench Verified run for Astra lands and goes the other way, we will revisit.
