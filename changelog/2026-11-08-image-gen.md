OpenAI announced Images 2.6 on 4 November, promising faster generation, more precise editing and new API controls. Four days later the new models landed on Artificial Analysis's text-to-image board: GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst (max) straight in at #1 on 1259, with GPT Image 2.6 Flare (max) #2 on 1228. That pushes our title holder, GPT-Image-2.5-Sunburst, down to #3 on 1227 — a 32-point deficit, well outside noise.

We are not moving the title yet. This is one board on one day, the first reading we have of these entries, and there is nothing to corroborate it: no second fetch showing the gap holding, no Arena text-to-image or single-image-edit numbers for either 2.6 variant, and no Artificial Analysis editing placement. Editability is one of this slot's four criteria, and much of 2.5 Sunburst's case rests on topping the AA editing board (1199) and both Arena boards. Until we can see whether 2.6 carries that across, a debut Elo is a strong signal rather than a settled one. The only other evidence is OpenAI's own launch post, which does not count as independent.

Practically, 2.5 Sunburst remains a sound default and the newer models are from the same vendor and API, so switching later should be cheap. We expect this to resolve quickly — if the Artificial Analysis gap holds on the next fetch, or Arena corroborates, the title moves to GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst.
