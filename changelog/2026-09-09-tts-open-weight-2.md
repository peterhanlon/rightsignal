Today's Artificial Analysis TTS standings put **BreezeBlue Breeze TTS 2 Open Weights** at #7 on 1215 Elo. Fish Audio S2 Pro, the current title holder, is no longer in the top ten at all — the figure recorded on this slot is 1128, some 87 points back. The earlier note on this card, that Breeze had dropped out of the top ten, is now out of date and has been corrected.

That is a lead on this slot's principal criterion, on the board we track, and it is consistent with the reading already logged against the challenger rather than a one-off spike. Combined with the incumbent's known weaknesses — a Fish Audio Research License that bars commercial deployment without a separate agreement, and downloadable weights a generation behind the API-only S2.1 Pro — the case for keeping the title where it is has thinned considerably.

I have stopped short of moving the title today for one reason: the dossier gives no official weights repository or licence statement for Breeze TTS 2 Open Weights, and licence and hardware requirements are stated criteria here. Naming a pick without a verifiable official repo and licence terms would be worse than a week's delay. Breeze's quoted ~100ms first-audio figure also assumes an H200-class GPU, the same practical barrier as the incumbent.

Step-Audio-EditX remains the pragmatic self-hosting option on paper — 12GB VRAM, Apache 2.0 repo code — but at 1104 Elo it is further behind, and its weights repo still carries no licence file. StepFun's StepAudio 2.5 TTS at #10 (1205) also outranks the pick, but is not confirmed open-weight in the standings.

Expect a change here as soon as Breeze's repo and licence are confirmed.
