Artificial Analysis's text-to-image board now puts GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst (max) first on 1,261, with the outgoing pick GPT-Image-2.5-Sunburst second on 1,229 and GPT Image 2.6 Flare (max) third on 1,227. That ordering was already in place when we first saw the board on 8 November and still holds on today's fetch, so it is not a one-day reading. The second AA board agrees: on image editing, 2.6 Sunburst leads on 1,209 from 6,226 votes, ahead of 2.6 Flare on 1,206 and the pick on 1,199, which settles the 'no editing data yet' reservation that kept 2.6 as a challenger last time.

The counter-evidence is LMArena, where the 2.5 model still leads single-image edit on 1,517 from 125,387 comparisons against a preliminary 1,476 for 2.6 Sunburst, and still tops Arena's text-to-image board. Preliminary rows are not settled contrary evidence, and AA's editing rank range for 2.6 Sunburst is 1–3, overlapping both 2.6 Flare and the outgoing pick — so treat the editing lead as narrow rather than decisive.

On availability and price there is nothing to separate them: AA lists $210.72 per 1,000 images for every 2.5 and 2.6 variant, so the upgrade costs nothing. Volume editing work is still far cheaper elsewhere — MAI-Image-2.6 at $38.91 per 1,000, with MAI-Image-2.7-Preview sitting at 1,195 on AA's editing board. If your workload is edit-heavy and Arena matches your taste better than AA, staying on 2.5 Sunburst remains defensible.
