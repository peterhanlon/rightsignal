Artificial Analysis's small open-models board changed hands today: Nanbeige4.3-33B entered at #1 with 306, pushing Qwen3.8 27B (Xhigh, 303) to second, with K2 Horizon MoVA 36B A4B new at #5 (281). Gemma 4 26B A4B, the current title holder, remains outside the top ten.

That is a quality signal, and only a quality signal. This slot is decided on quality per GB, tool-calling under quantisation and a verified 16GB fit, and for Nanbeige4.3-33B we have none of the latter two: no official 4-bit or GGUF build, no published file sizes, no retention figures against the full-precision checkpoint, and nothing on how tool-calling holds up once quantised. It is also a single board on a single day, with no second source and no history to show the placing holds. On the criteria as written, there is nothing yet to weigh against the pick's 14.2GB QAT int4 build and its documented function-calling support.

So it goes on the card as a challenger, not a replacement — joining Qwen3.8 27B, which has sat in much the same position for months: ahead on the index, short of a verified on-device package. The pattern is becoming the story of this slot. The board leaders are being published as GPU-oriented releases, and the laptop-sized builds arrive later from third parties without retention data.

If a 4-bit Nanbeige4.3-33B appears with published file sizes inside 16GB and credible tool-calling numbers under quantisation, it would be a strong candidate to take the title. Until then the pick stands, under review.
